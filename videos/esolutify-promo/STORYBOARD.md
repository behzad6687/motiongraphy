## Video direction

- **Five-layer stack in every scene:** gradient mesh (gold top-left, red bottom-right, drifting) → assets → graphics/type → soft-light gold grade → grain + vignette.
- **One hero colour per frame:** gold marks one word or element per frame. Red appears only in the logo and the "lose" / lost-lead moments. Green appears only on "Booked".
- **Rhythm:** hit → hold → build. Something moves within the first 15 frames of every scene, and nothing sits still longer than 90 frames.
- **Audio:** an original 120 BPM track (15 frames per beat) keeps every scene cut on a beat. It builds through the problem, drops on the logo, grooves through the middle and breaks down for the CTA. SFX land 2–3 frames before the visual hits.
- **Deliverable:** 1920×1080 H.264 MP4 at 30 fps, around 70s. A 1080×1920 vertical cut can reuse the same scenes.
