# Sol: character guide

Sol is eSolutify's AI agent, shown as a character: a small, bouncy sun wearing a receptionist headset. The name means sun, and the look matches the gold "Ask Sol" orb on esolutify.com. The model sheet is `renders/sol/sol-model-sheet.png` (Remotion still `SolModelSheet`).

## Who Sol is

- **Helpful, quick, a little cheeky.** He explains things in one line, then reacts.
- **Honest to a fault.** His running joke is "Full disclosure: I'm an AI. I'll be fair." He will say when a human is the better choice, which is what makes him believable.
- **Never smug for long.** If he gloats ("Ahem. That's me."), he gets taken down a peg in the next beat.

## How he looks and moves

| | |
|---|---|
| Body | Gold sun (`#F7D474` to `#C8973A`), 12 rounded rays, rosy cheeks, receptionist headset with mic |
| Moods | `happy`, `talk`, `laugh`, `shock` (rays spike), `smug`, `wink`, `worried` (+ sweat), `pout` |
| Props | Retro specs (the "since the 1960s" gag), sweat drop |
| Motion | Hops at the start of every line, squash and stretch, idle bob, blinks every ~3 s, waves |
| Voice | Cartoon babble (no words), one chirp per word. Speech bubbles carry the words, so it works muted. Generated by `scripts/gen_sol_audio.py` from `src/sol/script.json` |

## The format: "Sol explains" (about 40 s, 9:16)

1. **Hook, readable on frame 0:** one surprising number or claim from the post. Sol reacts in shock.
2. **"Hi, I'm Sol!"** plus the honest aside.
3. **Three short rounds** with game-show chips (ROUND 1, 2, 3), each with one picture, not a paragraph.
4. **A stamp line to remember**, for example "MINUTES, NOT MONTHS."
5. **The honest answer** (it depends, or "often both").
6. **CTA:** "Call Sol" (+1 365 360 3545) and "Read the full post", link in bio.

To make a new episode: copy `src/sol/script.json` (lines plus frames), rebuild the scene graphics in a new composition next to `SolExplainer.tsx`, then run `python3 scripts/gen_sol_audio.py` with the new script to regenerate the babble voice.
